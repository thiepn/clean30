# Clean30 Tester Guide

Thanks for testing Clean30.

Clean30 is a local-first apartment cleaning routine app. It helps with daily Tiny Rules, reset sessions, cleaning systems, customizable routines, history, and backups.

Live app: https://thiepn.github.io/clean30/

Recommended device: phone. Desktop testing is also useful.

## What To Test

- First launch and onboarding.
- Dashboard: can you tell what matters today?
- Tiny Rules: are the daily habits useful and quick?
- Start: can you start, pause, resume, finish partial, or discard a session?
- Systems: do the zone and priority explanations help?
- Routines: can you compare and prepare without feeling pushed to start?
- Customize: does Simple mode feel manageable? Is Advanced mode findable but not overwhelming?
- History: are completed entries understandable?
- Settings: do backup, privacy, help, and reset tools make sense?
- PWA install: can you add it to your phone home screen?

## Feedback Questions

- Was onboarding clear?
- Could you tell what to do from Dashboard?
- Were Tiny Rules useful?
- Was Start easy to use?
- Was Customize overwhelming?
- Did backup/privacy make sense?
- What confused you?
- Would you use this weekly?
- What felt unnecessary?

## Local-Only Data Note

Clean30 has no account, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no ads. Your data stays in your browser or installed PWA storage.

If you care about the test data you create, export a full backup from Settings. Clearing browser/app data may delete Clean30 data.
